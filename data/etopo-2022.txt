NOAA ETOPO 2022 surface elevations

Airwatch bundles approximate ground elevation grids for offline use. They
derive from NOAA NCEI's ETOPO 2022 global ice-surface relief model, distributed
under CC0. Source horizontal coordinates use WGS 84. Elevations are in meters
relative to the EGM2008 geoid.

Source documentation
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/etopo-global-relief-model
https://doi.org/10.25921/fd45-gt74

The grids use the 60-arc-second NetCDF data served by NOAA ERDDAP. Airwatch
keeps the native one-minute samples in the U.S. regions below and every fifth
row and column worldwide. The five-minute global grid is a coarse estimate,
and even the one-minute regional grids average terrain over an area. Values
can differ appreciably from local ground elevations, especially around
coastlines, ridgelines, and narrow valleys.

Download from
https://oceanwatch.pifsc.noaa.gov/erddap/griddap/ETOPO_2022_v1_60s.nc

The variable is z, with 10,800 latitude and 21,600 longitude samples.
The global request is z[0:5:10799][0:5:21599]. For a regional grid, request
z[(south+90)*60:1:(north+90)*60-1][(west mod 360)*60:1:(east mod 360)*60-1],
evaluating the expressions and percent-encoding the brackets.

U.S. regional grids are concatenated in this order. Bounds are in degrees
south, north, west, east. Row-major storage starts at the southern edge and
uses increasing longitude within each row. Each first sample is 1/120 degree
from the relevant south or west edge, per NOAA's grid registration.

Contiguous United States          24   50  -125   -66
Alaska                            50   72  -180  -129
Western Aleutian Islands          50   55   172   180
Hawaii                            18   23  -161  -154
Puerto Rico and U.S. Virgin Is.   17   19   -68   -64
Guam and Northern Mariana Is.     13   21   144   147
American Samoa                   -15  -11  -173  -169

For the global grid, rotate the incoming longitude axis by 2,160 samples so
that it starts at 180 W rather than 0. The western and southern first nodes
retain the source half-minute offset, with subsequent nodes five minutes apart.

The source models seafloor as well as terrain. Airwatch removes offshore
samples using the Natural Earth land polygons already distributed in
public/geo/basemap.geojson. Rasterize the polygons and their interior rings
in sample-coordinate space at four times the output resolution, then sample
the center of each output cell. Round finite land elevations to whole meters
and encode them as little-endian signed 16-bit integers. Encode cells outside
the land mask as -32768 and gzip the result. The endpoint bilinearly
interpolates available land values and returns no estimate when the nearest
grid sample is masked. This preserves measured below-sea-level land values
without using seafloor heights as ground elevations.

Natural Earth land polygons (public domain)
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
See public/geo/SOURCES.md for Airwatch's basemap provenance.
