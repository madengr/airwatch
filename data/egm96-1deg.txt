EGM96 geoid undulations, worldwide at 1 degree

The original data is the U.S. National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency's public-domain
EGM96 geoid grid, distributed by the OSGeo PROJ-data project as a 15-minute GeoTIFF.

NGA model information  https://earth-info.nga.mil/?action=wgs84&dir=wgs84
Source GeoTIFF         https://cdn.proj.org/us_nga_egm96_15.tif
PROJ-data provenance   https://github.com/OSGeo/PROJ-data/blob/master/us_nga/us_nga_README.txt

The source uses point samples with its first row at 90 N and first column at
180 W. Keep every fourth row and column, round heights from metres to
centimetres, and encode as little-endian signed 16-bit integers in row order.
For example, with Pillow and NumPy, the conversion of the source GeoTIFF is
`np.rint(np.asarray(Image.open(source), dtype=np.float64)[::4, ::4] * 100).astype('<i2')`.

The resulting grid has 181 latitude rows running north to south and 360
longitude columns running west to east. The antimeridian is periodic. Airwatch
bilinearly interpolates the retained nodes and subtracts the geoid undulation
from the reported WGS 84 ellipsoid height to estimate height above mean sea level.
Subsampling is suitable for display bands in thousands of feet.
